## From First-Year Coach to First Upset

Drew Gordon didn't arrive at La Salle in 2006 with a grace period. In his debut season as head coach, he took the Explorers to the Catholic Red title game at Northeast's Charlie Martin Memorial Stadium and beat St. Joseph's Prep — winners of 48 consecutive Catholic Red division games dating to 1999 — 14-7. The Hawks had been upset in just two other finals that decade, both to Cardinal O'Hara in 2000 and 2004. La Salle made it three.

{{PhotoInline id="gordon-portrait"}}

Linebacker JB Campanella made 13 tackles and combined with John McBurnie on a fourth-down sack. Defensive tackle Greg Frantz scooped up a fumble and returned it 96 yards for a touchdown — a play that, against any other opponent, becomes the postgame story by itself. On this night, it was merely a chapter.

> "We practice picking up fumbles every day. It was just bend down, pick it up and try to run all the way. I was praying no one would catch me. I'm not our fastest, but I got lucky tonight." — Greg Frantz

## The 2009 Snow Game at Hersheypark

Three seasons later, Gordon's Explorers finished the Catholic Red regular season with three straight losses. Then they flipped the switch. They won the CL Red championship, beat Washington 31-20 for the Class 4A City Title at Northeast behind Jamal Abdur-Rahman's two interceptions, 36-yard fumble-return touchdown, and 132 scrimmage yards — and then went to Hersheypark Stadium for a state-final date with State College.

{{PhotoInline id="gordon-sideline"}}

It snowed all day. Organizers moved kickoff from 7 p.m. to 2 p.m. The white stuff was still cascading down when the final horn sounded and the Explorers celebrated a 24-7 win — the Catholic League's first state championship in a large-enrollment major sport in just its second year of PIAA membership. Mike Bennett's 37-yard field goal tied the city career record with 26 field goals, matching Pat Kaiser's mark. Abdur-Rahman, Tim Wade, and Sam Feleccia all ran for touchdowns (Feleccia broke a 55-yarder on extended running duty after Abdur-Rahman left with a knee injury). Drew Loughery went 5-for-11 for 109 yards. Steve Sinnott forced and recovered a fumble. Vinny Migliarese picked off a pass.

## The 2012 City Title — Records Everywhere

Gordon's dynasty kept producing championships, but the 2012 City Title game against Frankford was different even by his standards. In a 37-20 win at Northeast, receiver Sean Coleman caught 14 passes (breaking the city postseason record of 12) for 138 yards and four touchdowns. Quarterback Chris Kane went 31-for-46 for 328 yards and four scores — the 31 completions and 46 attempts both city postseason records. Backup running back Jordan Meachum, who had missed most of the season with a hip injury, ran seven times for 146 yards and a touchdown, setting up a field goal and another score. It was the second overtime final in Catholic League history (the other was in 1990).

## The Big Picture — Nine Seasons, Ten Crowns

From 2006 through 2014, Drew Gordon's La Salle program went to nine consecutive Catholic Red title games, winning five Catholic League championships (2006, 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011), four City Titles (2009, 2010, 2011, 2012), and the 2009 state championship. Jamal Abdur-Rahman ran for over 1,000 yards three times (1,767 in 2010 remains the program standard). Tim Wade added a 1,564-yard season in 2011. Connor Hoffman's 648 receiving yards in 2009 and Sean Coleman's four 1,000-yard receivers in five years reset the school's offensive books.
