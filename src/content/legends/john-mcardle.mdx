## Coach Mac Came Home

John McArdle was a Chestnut Hill Academy alumnus who spent 17 seasons coaching basketball at his alma mater — from 1986-87 through 2002-03 — and then kept umpiring high school baseball in the neighborhoods around it for years afterward. He was, by every description from the CHA community who knew him, a teaching presence first and a game-planner second.

## The Three Co-Championships

McArdle's CHA teams reached the Inter-Ac summit three times during his 17 seasons: 1991-92 (8-2 league, 17-8 overall), 1993-94 (8-2 league, 19-4 overall), and 1997-98 (8-2 league, 20-8 overall) — all co-championships shared with the other top Inter-Ac program of that year.

His 1993-94 squad was his best on an overall record basis: 19-4 against a schedule that included the full Inter-Ac gauntlet plus non-league games. His 1997-98 team rolled up 20 wins and the third Inter-Ac co-title of his tenure.

## The Baseball Umpire

What almost nobody outside Chestnut Hill knew was that McArdle's second act was umpiring. Former players would see him behind the plate at high school baseball games for years after he stepped away from the basketball bench. He loved it. He was good at it. And to CHA graduates — the ones who wrote in after his death — the sight of Coach Mac calling balls and strikes at the bottom of the Wissahickon Inn lawn was one of the defining images of their school.
