2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Create an account: name, email address, username, and password.
  • Subscribe to a school or player: your preferences and associated email address.
  • Submit a correction or nomination: player name, school, sport, and any notes you provide.
  • Post a comment: your display name, comment text, and any other content you submit.
  • Contact us: your name, email address, and message content.
  • Participate in polls or community features: your votes and responses (which may be anonymous or associated with your account).
  • Advertise with us: business name, contact information, and payment details (processed by third-party payment processors; we do not store full payment card data).

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Site, we and our service providers may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Log data: IP address, browser type, browser version, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, time and date of visits, and time spent on pages.
  • Device information: hardware model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: session identifiers, preferences, and analytics data. See Section 4 for more information.
  • Usage data: search queries, filter selections, pages viewed, links clicked, and other interactions with the Site.

2.3 Information About Athletes and Public Figures

Our Site maintains a historical and editorial database of Philadelphia-area high school athletes and their careers. This database includes:

  • Names, schools attended, graduation years, and positions;
  • Statistical records compiled from publicly available sources;
  • Career information including college attendance, draft information, and professional team affiliations;
  • Awards, honors, and historical records;
  • Recruiting information from publicly available scouting databases.

This information is compiled from public records and publicly available sources for editorial, educational, and historical purposes. See Section 8 for more information about athlete data and requests.

  • Legal compliance: complying with applicable laws, regulations, legal process, and governmental requests.

We will not use your personal information for purposes materially different from those described in this Privacy Policy without providing you with prior notice and, where required by law, obtaining your consent.
